Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 07, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use CheckInn, how that data is used, and how it is protected.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and authentication-related information.
  • Business data: hotel, room, and reservation records entered in the app.
  • Technical data: device type, app version, crash logs, and basic usage analytics.

2. How We Use Data

  • To provide app services and manage user accounts.
  • To respond to support requests and resolve technical issues.
  • To improve security, performance, and product quality.

3. Data Sharing

Personal data is not sold to third parties. Infrastructure providers (for example authentication or cloud data services) may process data only as needed to deliver app functionality.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained while your account is active, or as required by applicable law. Upon account deletion requests, data is removed except where legal retention is required.

5. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect data. However, no internet transmission can be guaranteed as fully risk-free.

6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data. You can contact us using the details below.
